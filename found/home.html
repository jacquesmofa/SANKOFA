Objective

Celebrate and honor the invaluable contributions of individuals within the African community while raising funds to establish a more expansive platform that enhances accessibility and provides increased aid to community members.

Mission

The Canadian and African Women Aid Program has recognized a concerning lack of celebration and representation of the black community across various sectors. To address this issue, we are proud to announce the Sankofa Royale Awards—an initiative aimed at acknowledging and honoring the exceptional achievements of African individuals in diverse fields. By highlighting the outstanding contributions of these individuals, our goal is to shine a light on their invaluable impact on the community, foster inspiration, and growth, and unleash the untapped potential inherent in every black individual. Together, we will celebrate our community and its remarkable accomplishments.

Award Night

Experience the elegance of the Ambience Royale Fundraising Gala! Step onto the red carpet at our glamorous event and immerse yourself in an evening of pure luxury. Delight in captivating performances by talented local artists and indulge in a delectable array of African cuisine! Our esteemed award nominees will be treated to a red carpet-inspired paparazzi experience, while all guests can capture unforgettable moments amidst a splendid royale gala-themed backdrop.

As a fundraising gala, we deeply value any contributions made to not only support this exceptional project but also pave the way for future endeavors. Your generosity is sincerely appreciated, making a lasting impact for years to come.

Meaning of Sankofa

Sankofa, an African word originating from the Akan tribe in Ghana holds profound meaning. Its literal translation, “it is not taboo to fetch what is at risk of being left behind,” conveys the idea that we are not only allowed but encouraged to reflect on the past and bring its wisdom with us. By embracing this saying, we understand the importance of delving into our history to gain a profound appreciation for our culture and traditions. Through this introspection, we can pave the way for a brighter future for ourselves and future generations.

Knowing our history and heritage is integral to understanding our present selves and the world around us. It empowers us to make positive progress and shape a better future. Sankofa serves as a reminder that our past shapes our identity, and without recognizing and comprehending it, we cannot move forward. As members of the African community, we are encouraged to honor and celebrate our past, which forms an indelible part of our collective identity. We can draw upon this knowledge to guide our path forward and foster the flourishing of the entire African community.

Therefore, in this prestigious award ceremony, we come together to acknowledge and celebrate our rich heritage and culture. We also pay tribute to those individuals who contribute to the growth and development of our community. By doing so, we unite as a community, looking ahead with determination and dedication to construct a brighter future for all.

About CAWAP​

The Canadian and African Women Aid Program is a non-profit organization dedicated to empowering racialized Canadian and African women and their families. We are committed to enhancing their potential and well-being through educational, informative, and empowering programs and services. Our organization actively promotes a sense of belonging, community participation, and social interactions, while combating systemic racism and hunger.

We operate our programming through multi-service programming in six cluster areas:

  1. Women empowerment initiatives
  2. Youth recreational and after-school program
  3. Senior recreational and intergenerational programs
  4. Social enterprise and income generation
  5. Volunteerism and community engagement
  6. Income tax and financial literacy
  7. Summer Camp
  8. Summer jobs for youth/ seasonal youth jobs
  9. Skills Training
  10. Food Pantry
  11. Mental health
  12. Homeless and hot table
